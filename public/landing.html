Questions.

Is the Arabic text verified?

The Mushaf is set in the King Fahd Complex’s own page fonts, as published by Quran.com, so every page matches the printed Madinah Mushaf. We never edit the text.

Who owns what I write?

You do. Export any page whenever you like. Your notes are not used to train anything.

Can I study with other people?

Yes. Invite anyone to a workspace; you will see each other’s cursors and writing as it happens.

Does it cost anything?

No, and it will stay that way. There is no paid tier.
