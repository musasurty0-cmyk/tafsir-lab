SURAH AL-FĀTIḤA · 1:2

On praise as the opening posture of the believer

Y Yahya · last edit just now 5 collaborators
AL-FĀTIḤA · 1:2 ⌘ Bookmark ↗ Open drawer
ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ
Al-ḥamdu lillāhi rabbi'l-ʿālamīn.
2All praise is due to Allah, Lord of all the worlds.

The verse opens with al-ḥamd — definite, total — not merely thanks for what is given but praise for what He is. Al-Rāzī ties this directly to ↦ 1:1: the bismillāh names Him; al-ḥamd then renders Him His due.

A second reading places the lām of lillāh as the lām of istiḥqāq — entitlement — so the sentence does not describe a transaction but a fact of the universe.

— 02 The Mushaf

Ink that doesn’t
belong to the device.

The full Madinah Mushaf, page-faithful and zoomable. Annotate with pressure-sensitive ink, tag āyāt with juzʾ markers, drop sticky notes in the margin. Your ink is bound to the verse, not the page — it follows you to every reader who opens the same workspace.

  • ↳Pen, highlighter, and stamp tools.
  • ↳Layered annotations — toggle teacher’s ink on or off.
  • ↳Stylus-grade input — Apple Pencil, S-Pen, Wacom.
  • ↳Print-perfect export. Send a marked-up juzʾ to your students.

— 03 Workspaces

Every workspace is a folder of surahs, pages, and notes — owned by one person, by a class, or by a community. Invite by link, by domain, or keep a workspace strictly between you and the page.

  • ↳Three permission tiers — owner, editor, reader.
  • ↳Per-surah branching — each surah is its own document.

  • ↳Read-only publish — share a finished tafsir as a public page.

— Honest questions

A few things people ask.

Is the Arabic text verified?

Yes. Our base text is the Madinah Mushaf, cross-checked against tanzil.net. We do not edit the rasm or the diacritical marks under any circumstance.

Which tafāsīr do you ship?

Sixty-seven — twelve in English and fifty-five in Arabic. Al-Ṭabarī, al-Qurṭubī, al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Saʿdī, al-Baghawī, the Jalālayn, Ibn ʿĀshūr, al-Shawkānī, and many more — each indexed verse-by-verse and filterable by language in the drawer.

Who owns the notes I write?

You do. Export everything as Markdown + PNG at any time. We don’t train models on your study notes.

How is multiplayer different from a Google Doc?

Verse blocks are atomic. You can’t accidentally chop an āyah in half mid-merge. Comments anchor to verse references, not to character offsets, so they survive any rewrite.
