# Storyboard — one surface, twelve beats

Every beat lives in the single `#scene` clip. `src:` is `index.html` for all of
them: they are staged children of one continuous paper surface, not separate
compositions. Onsets are transcript-measured, not authored.

## Frame 1 — Live this life
status: outline · src: index.html · 0.00–2.72
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (rise-stagger entrance) + asr-keyword-glow
"Live this life, live this dunya." Words rise off a baseline in a tight stagger.
"dunya" carries a small gloss — *this world* — set beneath it.

## Frame 2 — First of all,
status: outline · src: index.html · 2.72–4.00
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (snap entrance) + discrete-text-sequence (ordinal)
Ordinal **01** snaps onto the rail under the words it belongs to.

## Frame 3 — as if you are a traveller
status: outline · src: index.html · 4.00–6.60
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (rise) + css-marker-patterns (marker sweep)
"as if you are a traveller, just simply…" — an ochre marker sweeps left to
right under "traveller," as he says it. The mark travels, which is the point.

## Frame 4 — مُجَرَّد مُسَافِر
status: outline · src: index.html · 6.60–7.94
rules: scale-swap-transition + spring-pop-entrance
"traveller." scales up and hands the centre to the Arabic, which springs in at
the same point. Transliteration and gloss draw underneath.

## Frame 5 — And secondly,
status: outline · src: index.html · 7.94–8.86

## Frame 7 — Make it so obvious
status: outline · src: index.html · 13.58–15.98
rules: asr-keyword-glow + spring-pop-entrance (one-shot scale on the held word)
He holds "so" for 1.14s. It snaps larger the instant he lands on it and then
does not move at all until he lets go — a slow crawl across a held word would
shimmer, and stillness reads as a man pausing anyway.
