# Storyboard — one surface, eleven beats

Every beat lives in the single `#scene` clip. `src:` is `index.html` for all of
them: they are staged children of one continuous paper surface, not separate
compositions. Onsets are transcript-measured, not authored: a beat runs from
its first word's onset to the next beat's first word's onset, derived from the
grid rather than typed. Frame 2 is the one exception — a ring needs a moment to
draw before he speaks to it.

Under the whole thing, the nasheed — 16.3 LU below his voice, fading in over
2.4s and out over 2.56s, only properly audible in the silence at Frame 10.

## Frame 1 — Live this life
status: outline · src: index.html · 0.00–2.72
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (rise-stagger entrance) + asr-keyword-glow
"Live this life, live this dunya." Words rise off a baseline as he says them —
the opening beat has no predecessor to roll off, so the speech is its entrance.
This is also where the train was: it is broadband and only ever audible in the
two gaps between these words, and a downward expander now takes 39–41 dB off
them without touching a syllable.

## Frame 2 — ①
status: outline · src: index.html · 2.72–4.00
rules: svg-path-draw (the ring) + spring-pop-entrance (the digit)
A ring draws itself from twelve o'clock and the numeral 1 lands inside it. He
is saying "First of all," — the numeral says it faster and does not need the
words.

## Frame 3 — مُجَرَّد مُسَافِر
status: outline · src: index.html · 4.00–7.94
rules: spring-pop-entrance + css-marker-patterns (the rule under the phrase)
The card is up for the whole run-in, not just the moment he names it: he starts
describing the idea at 4.00 ("as if you are a traveller, just simply…") and
only says the Arabic at 6.60. So the phrase is on screen while he circles it,
and the card's second beat is the transliteration landing at 6.52 — the instant
the words leave his mouth — with the gloss on the breath after. No other
captions in this stretch.

## Frame 4 — ②
status: outline · src: index.html · 7.94–8.90
rules: svg-path-draw + spring-pop-entrance
The same ring, the same landing, a different digit. The rhyme is the structure.

## Frame 5 — no reason
status: outline · src: index.html · 8.90–13.54
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (side-snap) + css-marker-patterns (marker sweep)
"when you live this life, give Allah no reason not to show you His face."
An ochre marker sweeps under "no reason" as he says it.

## Frame 6 — Make it so obvious
status: outline · src: index.html · 13.54–16.72
rules: asr-keyword-glow + spring-pop-entrance (one-shot scale on the held word)
He holds "so" for 1.12s. It snaps larger the instant he lands on it and then
does not move at all until he lets go — a slow crawl across a held word would
shimmer, and stillness reads as a man pausing anyway.

## Frame 7 — everything I've done for you is
status: outline · src: index.html · 16.72–17.94
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (rise) + asr-keyword-glow
Fast, low, a run-up — this beat exists to hand off to the Arabic.

## Frame 8 — تَعْظِيمُ اللهِ لِأَنَّهُ الله
status: outline · src: index.html · 17.94–19.22
rules: spring-pop-entrance + css-marker-patterns
The second card. Rhymes with Frame 3, but where that one waited two and a half
seconds for its transliteration, this one builds straight through — he says the
phrase the moment it appears. It also carries "Ya Allah," properly now: Frame 6
used to end on that word's ONSET rather than its end, and cleared it off screen
a beat before he said it.

## Frame 9 — to venerate you
status: outline · src: index.html · 19.22–22.40
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (rise-rotate) + asr-keyword-glow
The English of the phrase just shown, so the Arabic is understood in retrospect.

## Frame 10 — Not for anything in return. (and the silence)
status: outline · src: index.html · 22.40–25.20
rules: asr-keyword-glow + static hold
He stops at 23.92 and says nothing for 1.3s. The rule stops with him and the
frame goes completely still; the only thing left is the nasheed, which is why
it is there. Nothing moves until he speaks again.

## Frame 11 — so that He has no excuse
status: outline · src: index.html · 25.20–28.16
rules: kinetic-beat-slam (scale-slam finale) + spring-pop-entrance
"So that Allah has no excuse but to show you His face." "His face" lands
largest, alone on its line, and holds to the last frame.
