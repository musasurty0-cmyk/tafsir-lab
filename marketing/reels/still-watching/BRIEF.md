## What was done to the sound (cut.py)

- **Trimmed in** at "Tawheed means...": the clip opens on the tail of the
  previous sentence and then "And finally,", which makes the viewer feel they
  have missed the start.
- **Trimmed out** after "...is still watching you." The two words after it,
  "'ibad Allah", are the only Arabic in the clip and open his next section.
  Checked by asking the recogniser to DETECT the language over every 3s
  window, not by forcing English -- forced English translates Arabic.
- **One false start removed**: "If our intention is... if our intention
  becomes wrong". The recogniser dropped the first attempt entirely; it showed
  only as one word stretched to 1.7s.
- Gate, then 3:1 compression, then a limiter. The gate is there because the
  compressor's makeup gain would have lifted the hall between sentences above
  the nasheed. Speech probes are unchanged within 0.1 dB.
- The render lands at -12.1 LUFS, beside the sibling's -12.0.
