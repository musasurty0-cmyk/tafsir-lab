al-ʿibādatu lā tusammā ʿibādatan illā maʿa at-tawḥīd
worship is not called worship except with tawḥīd
kamā anna aṣ-ṣalāta lā tusammā ṣalātan illā maʿa aṭ-ṭahārah
just as prayer is not called prayer except with purity
lā ilāha illā Allāh
there is no god but Allah
innamā l-aʿmālu bi-n-niyyāt
Verily, actions are by their intentions.
wa-innamā li-kulli mri’in mā nawā
And every person will have what they have intended.
